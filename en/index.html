Fitup Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

1. Purpose of Collection and Use

Fitup ("the Company") collects and uses personal information for the following purposes:

  • Account registration and management, and role-based service (member, instructor, branch manager)
  • Class reservations, attendance management (QR / walk-in), and coupon management
  • Health and body-composition (InBody) records, and meal recommendations and intake logging
  • Community services (posts, comments, photos, videos) and safety moderation
  • Member-to-member messaging (chat)
  • Push notifications and meal reminders
  • Customer support and dispute resolution

3. Processing of Health, InBody, and Diet Information

The Company stores health and body-composition data that you enter or that is extracted via optical character recognition (OCR) from an InBody result-sheet photo, together with your meal recommendations, actual intake, food-search, and water-intake logs, in your account to provide health-management features. Health and body-composition data may constitute sensitive information; the Company collects and uses it only where you have entered and consented to it in order to use the relevant feature, and uses it solely for the purpose of providing health-management features.

⚠️ Medical disclaimer: The health, nutrition, and meal information and recommendations provided by Fitup are for general informational and reference purposes only and are not medical diagnosis, prescription, or treatment. Always consult a physician or other qualified professional for health-related decisions.

Health and diet information is shown only to you, and is deleted when you delete your account (see Section 11).

4. Community Content and File Storage

Posts, comments, images, and videos you publish in the community may be visible to other users. Attached image and video files are stored on Cloudflare R2.

  • Permanent post deletion: When you or an administrator delete a post, the post, comments, and likes are removed, and the image and video files stored on R2 are permanently deleted as well.
  • Blocking: Posts from blocked users are immediately removed from your feed.

5. Messaging (Chat)

  • Leaving a conversation: For a conversation with a user who is still active, "Leave" hides it only from your own list (it remains in the other person's list) and may reappear when a new message arrives.
  • Withdrawn-user conversations: A conversation whose other party has deleted their account can be permanently deleted.

6. Push Notifications and Meal Reminders

The Company sends push notifications such as reservation, attendance, community, and meal reminders. Some notifications, including meal reminders, are sent only if you opt in, and can be turned off at any time in the app or device settings.

7. Subprocessors and Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not provide personal information to third parties without your consent. To deliver the service, the Company uses the following subprocessors:

  • Google Firebase (auth, database, push): account authentication, data storage, notifications
  • Cloudflare R2: storage of community image and video files
  • Apple / Google: in-app purchase processing
  • Google AdMob: in-app advertising (see Section 8)

Personal information may otherwise be disclosed where you have consented in advance, where required by law, or to provide member information to the branch manager/instructor of your branch for service delivery.

8. Advertising

The Company may display in-app advertisements through Google AdMob to operate the service.

  • The Company currently serves non-personalized advertising that does not track users.
  • Advertising-related data, such as the advertising identifier, may be processed in accordance with Google AdMob's policies.
  • You can limit ad personalization (interest-based ads) in your device settings.
    ― Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
    ― iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • For more on how Google processes advertising data, see the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

10. Children Under 14

This service is available only to users who are 14 years of age or older. The Company does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. The Company verifies age via date of birth at sign-up, and registration is restricted for anyone determined to be under 14. If the Company learns that it has collected personal information from a child under 14, it will delete such information without delay.

11. Data Deleted and Retained Upon Account Withdrawal

Deleted: Upon withdrawal, your profile, health/InBody/diet records, community posts/comments/attachments (including on R2), chats, notifications, and attendance/reservation/coupon data are deleted.

Retained: Accounting records such as payment and settlement may be retained for the periods below, in a separated/anonymized form, where retention is required by applicable law.

  • Records on contracts or withdrawal of subscription: 5 years
  • Records on payment and supply of goods: 5 years
  • Records on consumer complaints or dispute handling: 3 years

12. Role-Based Data Processing

  • Member: processes your own reservation, attendance, coupon, health, diet, and community data.
  • Instructor: processes attendance information for assigned classes and enrolled members.
  • Branch manager: processes the operations, member, and settlement information of the branch. Upon a branch manager's withdrawal, store data is deleted or retained depending on the store status.

13. Reporting and Administrator Review Policy

Reports of community content are handled as follows:

  • Reported content is hidden from the reporter's own feed.
  • Severe types such as sexual or illegal content are temporarily hidden (reversible) pending review, preventing public exposure.
  • The final deletion of content and suspension of users are decided by administrator review and judgment (content is not automatically deleted based on a single report).
  • If a report is found to be false, the content is restored and the false reporter may be subject to penalties.

14. Destruction of Personal Information

When the retention period expires or the processing purpose is achieved, the Company destroys the personal information without delay.

15. Your Rights

You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information, via the in-app settings or customer support.

16. Technical Measures for Protection

  • Encryption of personal information and protection in transit
  • Technical measures against hacking and intrusion
  • Access restriction and privilege management
